Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form, message us on WhatsApp or call. Tell us the product, size, quantity and where it's needed, and we'll come back with stock and price.

Do you deliver, and how much does it cost?

Delivery can be arranged on request. Tell us your site location when you ask for a quote and we'll confirm availability and the delivery cost together with your price.

Can you cut steel to a specific length?

Yes, cut-to-size is available. Include the lengths you need in your request so we can price it correctly.

Can you fabricate from my drawings?

Yes. Send drawings, sketches or clear photos with dimensions on WhatsApp and we'll review the job and quote it.

How do I pay?

Payment arrangements are confirmed with your quote. Ask us about accepted payment methods and terms for larger orders.
